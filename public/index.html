What just happened

The button hit a paid route with no payment. The server answered 402 with an accepts array holding both rails — USDC on Base and USDC on Solana. The modal read that challenge, had your wallet sign the matching payment, and retried with X-PAYMENT. The facilitator verified and settled, and the signed confirmation came back in the 200 body with the receipt in X-PAYMENT-RESPONSE. Nothing was reserved, shipped, or charged beyond the micropayment.

The modal also remembers you via SIWX (sign-in-with-X) so a second purchase skips the wallet prompt, and it enforces spending caps client-side. It is a proprietary npm package loaded here from the CDN — see @three-ws/x402-payment-modal.

Agents skip the modal entirely: GET /town for the directory, then pay with x402-fetch or any x402 client. See skill.md and /.well-known/x402.
